## Research Interests

- Computer Vision
- Autonomous Driving
- Virtual Try-on

<br>

**-Multi-Modal autonomous driving upstream tasks** It focuses on the precision and efficiency challenges in multi-modal perception tasks. I have conducted research on 3D semantic occupancy prediction conditioned on camera and LiDAR inputs, BEV semantic segmentation using multi-view cameras, and multi-frame monocular depth estimation with efficiency-oriented cue fusion.

**-Image-based virtual try-on visual tasks:** It revolved around visual realism and representation form issues. I have proposed a progressive pipeline of virtual try-on, a learning paradigm of garment transfer through cross-task knowledge
distillation, an unsupervised synthesis method of garment images, and an image sequence inference method via pose interpolation.
